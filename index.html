FAQ / Help

Have questions? We’ve got answers.

How long are the programs?

It depends on the program and your goals. Tell us what you need and we confirm duration when you enquire.

Are they online, offline or hybrid?

We confirm the format for each cohort. College workshops are delivered on campus as a full day or two half-days.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. The Corporate Ready Graduate workshop includes a SkillYugma Certificate of Completion.

What about pricing, batch size and refunds?

These are shared when you enquire, so they match the program and group.

Do I get support after the session?

Our team reviews LinkedIn profiles and supports internships and placements for workshop students.
